# Career and Professional Writing QA

- [ ] Every material candidate claim is bound to resume, profile, or accomplishment evidence.
- [ ] Every recipient-specific statement is bound to a public or first-party relationship source.
- [ ] The artifact is adapted to recipient role, relationship, channel, and intent.
- [ ] The opening establishes specific relevance; it is not a name/company mail merge.
- [ ] One concrete proof point supports the requested action.
- [ ] The CTA is clear, low-friction, and appropriate for the relationship.
- [ ] Length follows the channel profile in `config/artifacts.yaml`.
- [ ] No blocked personal data, invented facts, generic flattery, or corporate filler.
- [ ] The RDW receipt is content-bound and says `approved_for_human_review`.
- [ ] A human still reviews and controls sending, submission, or publication.
