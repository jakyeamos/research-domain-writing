# Career and Professional Writing Templates

These are structures, not copy-and-paste prose. Populate each evidence slot before drafting.

## outreach_email

Subject: short role/company or shared-context cue.

1. Specific reason for contacting this recipient, grounded in recipient or role evidence.
2. One candidate proof point that maps directly to that reason.
3. A low-friction question or offer to share useful context.
4. Optional approved proof link and a simple sign-off.

Recipient emphasis:

- Recruiter: screening fit and verifiable qualifications.
- Hiring manager: team problem and adjacent demonstrated impact.
- Peer: shared work or topic; do not lead with a referral request.
- Interviewer: researched context and preparation; do not re-pitch the application.

## professional_message

Use the same relevance -> proof -> low-friction CTA structure, compressed to the destination's character budget. A connection note should start a conversation, not contain the whole application pitch.

## resume_bullet

Action and scope -> measurable or observable result -> method or constraint. Bind every number and named outcome to candidate evidence.

## cover_letter

Role-specific thesis -> two evidence-backed matches -> why this team or problem -> concise close. Do not repeat the resume chronologically.

## application_answer

Answer the exact question first -> support with a bounded example -> connect back to the role. Respect the form's word or character limit.
